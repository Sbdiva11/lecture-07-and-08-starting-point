Q1

Give your page a visual style

Open response · 50 points

Add a linked CSS file to your Homework 1 page. Use the checklist below to make design choices that are both intentional and easy to see.

  • 01 Link an external stylesheet. Add a <link rel="stylesheet"> tag in the <head> of your HTML file.
  • 02 Use three selector types. Include a type selector (p), a class selector (.classname), and an ID selector (#id).
  • 03 Show the box model on at least three elements. Deliberately adjust margin, padding, and border to create space and separation.
  • 04 Choose at least two complementary colors. Apply them to text, backgrounds, or borders.
  • 05 Make it feel finished. Aim for consistent spacing, color, and typography—not the browser defaults.
HOW YOU’LL BE GRADED

Grading rubric

50 PTS

Linked CSS file

10 pts
  • Full credit · 10 pt CSS is linked in the head and visibly styles the page.
  • No credit · 0 pt No external CSS, or styles are inline or in a style tag.

Three selector types

10 pts
  • Full credit · 10 pt Type, class, and ID selectors are correctly written and applied.
  • Partial · 6 pt Two types are correct, or one of the three has a syntax error.
  • No credit · 0 pt Only one type is used, or selectors do not apply visible styles.

Box model demonstrated

15 pts
  • Full credit · 15 pt Three or more elements use deliberate margin, padding, and border.
  • Partial · 8 pt The box model appears on one or two elements, or all three properties are not used together.
  • No credit · 0 pt No meaningful box-model styling; spacing remains at browser defaults.

Color scheme

10 pts
  • Full credit · 10 pt At least two complementary colors are applied intentionally.
  • Partial · 5 pt Only one custom color, or colors clash or feel random.
  • No credit · 0 pt Only browser-default black, white, or blue.

Overall visual coherence

5 pts
  • Full credit · 5 pt Consistent font, spacing, and colors create a unified style.
  • No credit · 0 pt Styling is minimal or inconsistent and the page looks unstyled.
